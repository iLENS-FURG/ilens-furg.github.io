## Internet through a Lens

iLENS is a research initiative within the **SYSTEMS Research Group** at the **Federal University of Rio Grande (FURG)** in Brazil.

We study how the Internet operates and evolves, with a focus on **measurement, routing, security, interconnection, network operations, and Internet governance**.

### What we do

Our research combines Internet measurement, data analysis, experiments, and collaboration with researchers, network operators, Internet organizations, and the wider technical community.

### Research areas

**Architecture · Routing · Security · Interconnection · Operations · Governance**

These areas often overlap. Our projects examine concrete Internet systems and operational questions across them.

### Part of SYSTEMS

iLENS is part of the SYSTEMS Research Group at FURG, which brings together research on computer and networked systems.

### Collaborations

We work with universities, Internet organizations, network operators, and technical communities in Brazil and internationally. Collaborating institutions and researchers are listed on the relevant project pages.
