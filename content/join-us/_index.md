## Work with us

iLENS is part of the **SYSTEMS Research Group at the Federal University of Rio Grande (FURG)**. We welcome students and researchers interested in Internet measurement, routing, security, interconnection, and network operations.

### Students

We work with undergraduate and graduate students on research projects involving Internet measurements, data analysis, experiments, and network operations.

### Visiting researchers

We are open to research visits and collaborations with researchers working on related Internet and networking topics.

### Research collaborations

We collaborate with universities, Internet organizations, network operators, and technical communities. If you have a research question, dataset, measurement platform, or operational problem that could benefit from joint investigation, get in touch.
