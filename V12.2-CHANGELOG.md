# iLENS Website V12.2

## Focus

Simplify the public website around the actual current goal: a professional research-group site that aggregates important information and is easy to maintain.

## Changes

- Simplified primary navigation to Research, Projects, People, Publications, Data & Software, About, and Join Us.
- Removed Observatories and Research Map from primary navigation.
- Removed research-system lifecycle as a dominant homepage element.
- Reworked homepage around research, projects, publications, people, outputs, and collaboration.
- Added Join Us page for students, visiting researchers, and collaborations.
- Simplified Research page to six public-facing research areas and featured projects.
- Simplified project pages while retaining role, description, team, collaborators, funding, research questions, outputs, and publications.
- Simplified project listing and removed unnecessary RQ/person counters.
- Simplified About page to institutional and research-group information.
- Simplified footer navigation.
- Added content-management documentation explaining canonical sources and what should be edited manually.
- Removed placeholder/example publication metadata and example news from the public information set.
- Kept planned observatories and deeper research-system entities available in the infrastructure without presenting them as mature public products.
- Updated generated site metadata/version counts.

## Philosophy

The website should look like an excellent research group with a strong information system behind it—not like a research-management platform exposed to visitors.
